# The Quality Work Studio website

Static site, no build step. 17 pages built from "The Quality Work Studio - Full Website Content" sheet.
URLs are clean on Vercel (/about, /services/qa-testing ...) via vercel.json.

## Deploy
`npx vercel --prod` from this folder (project: qualityworks-studio).

## Before launch
1. Contact form: create a form at formspree.io and replace `YOUR_FORM_ID` in contact.html.
2. Case Studies: page shows the format only. Add real projects when you have them.
3. Blog: article titles are marked "Coming soon". Add real articles later.
4. Privacy, Terms and Cookie pages are starter text, NOT legal advice. Have a legal professional review them and add your registered business details. Update the Cookie Policy if you add analytics or ads.
5. If your domain is not https://qualityworks-studio.vercel.app, update sitemap.xml, robots.txt and canonical/og tags.
