Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how The Quality Work Studio may collect, use, store and protect information when you use our website or contact us about our services.

Information We Collect

We collect information you choose to give us, such as your name, company, email address, phone number, selected service, budget range and project details when you contact us.

How We Use Information

We use the information to respond to enquiries, prepare proposals, deliver services, communicate with you and improve our website and services.

Contact and Enquiry Data

Details submitted through our contact form are delivered to us by our form provider and email. We use them only to respond to your enquiry and manage the working relationship.

Cookies and Analytics

Our website does not currently use advertising or analytics cookies. See our Cookie Policy. If this changes, we will update that policy.

Third-Party Services

We use third-party services to run the site and handle enquiries, including hosting, a form-handling provider, web fonts and email. These providers process data under their own policies.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information from unauthorised access, loss or misuse. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Data Retention

We keep enquiry and project information only as long as needed for the purposes above or to meet legal and accounting obligations.

Your Rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable law.

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
