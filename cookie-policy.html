Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how cookies and similar technologies may be used on The Quality Work Studio website.

What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by websites you visit. They can make a site work, remember choices or measure how it is used.

Essential Cookies

This website is a simple static site and does not currently set essential cookies of its own.

Analytics Cookies

We do not currently use analytics cookies. If we add analytics in future, we will list them here and ask for consent where required.

Advertising Cookies

We do not currently use advertising or tracking cookies on this website.

Third-Party Cookies

Embedded third-party services, such as web fonts, may receive technical information like your IP address when the page loads. We do not control their cookie practices.

Managing Cookie Preferences

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If we introduce non-essential cookies, a preference control will be added to this website.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy if our use of cookies changes. The latest version will always be on this page.
